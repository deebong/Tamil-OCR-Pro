About Tamil OCR Pro

This tool runs entirely in your browser, guaranteeing 100% privacy.

✨ Pro Features

  • Pixel-Perfect Spacing: Reconstructs paragraphs natively to match the original image.
  • Live Enhance Scan: Uses OpenCV math to eliminate shadows and paper textures in real-time.
  • Zero Server Costs: Since your computer does the processing, it's totally free with no limits.
  • Ghost Character Removal: Strips invisible ZWNJ Unicode artifacts so text pastes cleanly into Notepad/Word.

⚠️ Known Limitations

  • Handwriting: Not supported. The AI model is heavily optimized for printed text only.
  • Files: Only processes images (JPG, PNG, WEBP). Does not support multi-page PDFs or DOCX files.
  • Hardware: Heavy images may take longer to process on older mobile devices.
